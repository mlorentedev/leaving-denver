# Backup Exits Runbook: Donation, Recycling and Giveaway

What to do with whatever has not sold, before the household deadline of 2026-10-23. The car has its own runbook ([vehicle-sale.md](vehicle-sale.md)) and its own deadline, 2026-11-09. These are backups: sell first, then give things away. Every fact below was read on the organisation's own site on 2026-10-01, and the source is linked next to it. **UNVERIFIED** marks anything the official page did not confirm: call before counting on it. Owner steps are marked **[OWNER]**.

## The organisations

### The Good Couch (sofas, sectionals, sleepers)

- Takes upholstered seating, sleeper sofas included. Pickup is free in good condition: removable stains, some pet hair and one or two minor tears are fine. Otherwise it costs about $50–150. They carry it down stairs, and there is no fee to cancel. [donate-your-couch](https://www.thegoodusedcouch.com/donate-your-couch/)
- Serves "most of the Front Range including Denver". 80111 is not named explicitly: **UNVERIFIED**, so ask when booking. [FAQ](https://www.thegoodusedcouch.com/couch-donation-frequently-asked-questions/)
- **[OWNER]** Book with the site form, or text the photos to (720) 234-2068. The FAQ also lists (720) 869-7510. There is no tax receipt.

### ARC Thrift (Colorado)

- Free curbside pickup of **large furniture only**, Mon–Fri 8:00–17:00. Book on (303) 238-5263 (Mon–Fri 8–14) or with the form on [arcthrift.com/donations](https://arcthrift.com/donations).
  - The driver decides on the spot: "clean, immediately reusable, no stains, rips, tears or pet hair".
  - Mark every item "ARC" and leave it at ground level.
  - The lead time and the ZIP coverage are not published: **UNVERIFIED** for 80111.
- Refuses mattresses and box springs, TVs and monitors. [Items we cannot accept](https://arcthrift.us/ItemsWeCannotAccept.php)
- Nearest drop-offs:
  - 8100 S Quebec St, Centennial, (720) 440-6340;
  - 7450 S University Blvd (Dry Creek), (720) 728-6390.

### Habitat for Humanity Metro Denver ReStore

- Pickup costs **$40** per appointment, booked 2–3 business days out; the priority option is about 48 h. Book on [ReSupply](https://donor.resupply.cloud/start/charity/42). [restore/donate](https://habitatmetrodenver.org/restore/donate/) (an old blog post still says pickup is free; the donate page is current).
- Pickup zone: check the address on the [locations map](https://habitatmetrodenver.org/restore/locations/#pickupzone). 80111 coverage is **UNVERIFIED**.
- Free drop-off daily 10:00–17:30. The nearest store is in Aurora, 1640 S Abilene St.
- Refuses mattresses and airbeds, TVs, monitors, other electronics and appliances that do not work. It wants items in good condition, working, under 10 years old and complete. Particleboard is **UNVERIFIED**: likely refused.

### Goodwill of Colorado

- Free drop-off at staffed centres; paid pickup through ReSupply (about 48 h). [material donations](https://goodwillcolorado.org/donations/material-donations/)
- Refuses TVs, mattresses, box springs, pillows, and furniture that is broken, stained or worn. Monitors are **UNVERIFIED**.
- Nearest centre to 80111: check the locator.

### Electronics (TV and monitor)

- **Best Buy:** $29.99 per TV or monitor. Flat TVs up to 50"; at most 2 TVs and 3 items per household per day. [recycling](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/services/recycling/pcmcat149900050025.c?id=pcmcat149900050025)
- **Blue Star Recyclers:** 953 Decatur St, Denver 80204, Mon–Fri 9–17 and Sat 9–13. LCD screens $0.79/lb, no size limit. [for residents](https://bluestarrecyclers.org/electronics-recycling/for-residents/)
- **At Your Door (Waste Management):** doorstep electronics pickup, $20, serves Centennial and Greenwood Village, 1-800-449-7587. The lead time is **UNVERIFIED**. [Arapahoe County resources](https://www.arapahoeco.gov/your_county/about_arapahoe_county/community/trash_and_recycling_resources.php)
- The City of Centennial's collection day is Sat 2026-10-03, 8:00–11:00, at 13133 E Arapahoe Rd. That is too early for things still for sale, so it does not apply unless an item breaks. [event](https://www.centennialco.gov/Residents/Health-Environment/Electronic-Recycling-Event)

### Buy Nothing and Craigslist free

- **Buy Nothing:** use the app (it works by location) or the local Facebook group. Find yours on [buynothingproject.org/find-a-group](https://buynothingproject.org/find-a-group). Greenwood Village, Centennial and DTC may be separate groups. Pickup is usually from the porch.
- **Craigslist free:** the general [prohibited list](https://www.craigslist.org/about/prohibited) applies. Unsanitised bedding and recalled items are banned, and the same ad must not be posted in several areas.
- Give pickup by the door and a time window. Never post the unit number publicly; send it in a message once someone confirms.

### Used bedding (topper)

- Humane Colorado welcomes "blankets, towels and harnesses, new or used". The full list did not load, and foam is not mentioned: **UNVERIFIED**, probably not. Call (303) 751-5772 before taking the topper. [donation center](https://humanecolorado.org/donation-center/)
